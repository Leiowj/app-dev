# Favorite Series
**My favorite series is Sandman. It’s a dark fantasy story created by Neil Gaiman that combines mythology, dreams, and human emotions in a very unique way. The series follows Dream, also known as Morpheus, one of the seven Endless beings who represent powerful forces of existence. What I love about Sandman is how it blends imagination with deep themes about life, death, and destiny. Every episode feels mysterious and thought-provoking, making it one of the most creative and meaningful series I’ve ever watched.**
